# 🎚️ PRESET CHAINS POR GÉNERO (Resumen para STAR CHAIN)

## 🔴 TRAP UNDERGROUND (Luar La L, Dei V, Myke Towers)
- **Pitch:** Auto-Tune Speed 0-5, Chromatic, Heavy effect
- **Comp:** CLA-76 o 1176, Ratio 8:1, Attack fast
- **EQ:** HPF 60Hz, Cut 200-300Hz -3dB, Boost 3kHz +2dB, Boost 10kHz +1.5dB
- **Reverb:** Chamber small, Decay 1.2s, Mix 12-18%
- **Delay:** 1/8 note, Tape mode, Feedback 15-20%

## 🟡 REGGAETÓN MELODIC (Feid, Rauw, Karol G)
- **Pitch:** Auto-Tune Speed 10-15, Natural vibrato On
- **Comp:** LA-2A Compress mode, 40-50% Peak Reduction
- **EQ:** Boost 60-80Hz +2dB, Cut 300Hz -2dB, Boost 8-12kHz +2.5dB
- **Reverb:** VintageVerb 1980s, Decay 1.8-2.0s, Mix 20%
- **Delay:** 1/4 note, Mix 8-12%

## 🟠 TRAP ARGENTINO (Duki, Khea, Paulo Londra)
- **Pitch:** Auto-Tune Speed 5-10, Chromatic
- **Comp:** 1176, Input 55-65%, fast attack, Ratio 8:1
- **EQ:** HPF 80Hz, Cut 250Hz -3dB, Presence +5kHz +2dB
- **Reverb:** ValhallaRoom, Decay 1.5s, Mix 15%
- **Delay:** 1/4, Feedback 25%, High Cut 5kHz (oscurece)
- **Extra:** Saturn 2 Tape saturation 15-20%

## 🔵 CORRIDOS TUMBADOS (Peso Pluma, Natanael Cano)
- **Pitch:** Auto-Tune Speed 8-12, Scale = Major acorde
- **Comp:** Waves RVox o LA-2A suave
- **EQ:** Preserva cuerpo mid-range 500Hz-2kHz, cut mud 300Hz
- **Reverb:** UA Pure Plate, Decay 2.5s, Mix 22% (espacio grande)
- **Extra:** J37 Tape (warmth analógico)

## 🟢 R&B URBANO (Omar Courtz, Jhayco, Rauw)
- **Pitch:** Auto-Tune Speed 20-30, Humanize 60%, Flex-Tune On
- **Comp:** LA-2A suave, 35-45% Peak Reduction
- **EQ:** Mid/Side — boost 2kHz Mid +1.5dB, cut 300Hz Side -2dB, Air Maag 40kHz
- **Reverb:** Plate, Decay 2.0s, Pre-Delay 20ms, Mix 25%
- **Delay:** 1/4 note ghost delay Mix 8%

## ⚫ RKT / CUMBIA 420 (L-Gante)
- **Pitch:** Auto-Tune EFX+ Heavy, robotic
- **Comp:** CLA-76 All-buttons nuke
- **EQ:** Boost massive bass 60-80Hz +5dB, corte drástico 200Hz -5dB
- **Reverb:** ValhallaSupermassive Warp 70%, Mix 18%
- **Extra:** Saturn 2 Distortion heavy
